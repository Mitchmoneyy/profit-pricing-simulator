The formulas

Contribution per month = price × (1 − refund rate) − cost to serve − (price × fee% + $0.30)
Average lifetime (months) = 1 ÷ monthly churn, capped at 60
LTV = contribution per month × average lifetime
Retention after n months = (1 − monthly churn) ^ n

Typical monthly churn

Rough tiers for small and mid-sized SaaS: around 5% a month is average, 3% is top-quartile and 1.5% or less is top-10%. Memberships and creator subscriptions usually run higher, around 6–9%.

Frequently asked questions

What is a good churn rate for SaaS?

For small-business SaaS, 3–5% monthly is common and under 2% is excellent. Enterprise products often see well under 1% monthly.

Why does LTV use contribution instead of revenue?

Hosting, support and payment fees are paid every month the customer stays. Revenue-based LTV ignores them and overstates what a customer is worth.

What is CAC payback?

The month in which a customer's cumulative contribution, after churn, first exceeds what you paid to acquire them. Under 12 months is a common target.

Is monthly or annual churn better to track?

Track monthly for decisions, since it reacts faster. Annual retention equals (1 − monthly churn)^12.
